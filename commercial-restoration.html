COMMERCIAL PROPERTY

Recovery planning for the places your business depends on.

Water, fire or other property damage can affect staff, customers, tenants and daily operations. This concept page is designed to help commercial property decision-makers organise the first conversation and clarify the potential scope of work.

Discuss a commercial property ↗Documentation guide ↓
BUILT AROUND YOUR SITE

Coordinate the response around the property.

Service capacity, response times and building types supported must be confirmed with the company.

01 / ASSESS

Understand the impact

Identify affected areas, immediate safety concerns, access limitations and any operational priorities.

02 / COORDINATE

Align stakeholders

Clarify the point of contact for owners, facility managers, tenants, contractors and the insurer where relevant.

03 / DOCUMENT

Agree on updates

Ask how scope, schedule changes, site access, estimates and progress will be recorded and communicated.

PREPARE FOR THE FIRST CALL

Bring the key details together.

A clear initial brief helps everyone discuss the situation more efficiently.

  • Property type and location
  • Nature of the damage and when it was discovered
  • Areas affected and any access restrictions
  • Operational constraints or critical equipment
  • Insurance claim contact, if available
For property managers: Confirm the provider’s commercial experience, insurance, licences where applicable, service area, availability and written scope before authorising work. No response-time or capability guarantee is made on this concept page.
