OUR APPROACH

Glamville Wellness & Beauty Spas serves clients across Zambia with a broad range of beauty, body-care, and wellness experiences. From nail and hair services to facials, massages, and spa packages, the focus is on helping every guest find the care that suits them.

With locations across Kitwe, Ndola, Lusaka, and Solwezi, Glamville brings its approach to self-care closer to more communities.

This concept presents that story through a calmer, more editorial digital experience—with service discovery, branch information, and a clearer way to request an appointment.
