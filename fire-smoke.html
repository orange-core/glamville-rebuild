A PRACTICAL GUIDE

Three steps to help you get oriented.

Use these prompts to prepare for a conversation with a qualified restoration provider.

01

Keep safety first

Do not re-enter a fire-damaged building until the fire department or relevant authority says it is safe. Avoid affected utilities and follow official guidance.

02

Document visible damage

When safe, record affected areas and belongings. Keep receipts for urgent expenses and follow your insurer’s documentation requirements.

03

Discuss restoration options

Ask about assessment, soot cleanup, smoke-odour treatment, contents handling, structural protection and what the proposed scope includes.

Fire-damaged buildings can have hidden structural, electrical and air-quality hazards. Follow directions from emergency responders and qualified professionals.
