MOISTURE & MOLD CONCERNS

Address moisture concerns with a clear plan.

Persistent moisture can lead to mold growth and damage to building materials. A careful response starts with understanding the moisture source, the affected area and whether specialist assessment is needed.

A PRACTICAL GUIDE

Three steps to help you get oriented.

Use these prompts to prepare for a conversation with a qualified restoration provider.

01

Find the moisture source

Leaks, condensation and damp materials can contribute to mold growth. If safe, note where moisture appears and when it started.

02

Avoid disturbing affected areas

Do not dry-brush or disturb suspected mold. Keep people away from unsafe or extensive affected areas and seek qualified advice.

03

Ask about the remediation plan

Discuss containment, removal of affected materials, moisture correction, cleanup and how the provider assesses completion.
