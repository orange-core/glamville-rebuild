INSURANCE & DOCUMENTATION

Not sure what to ask your insurer?

Keep records of the damage and any communication. Ask your insurance provider about coverage, deductibles, approved vendors and the claims process. Coverage decisions are made by your insurer.

Helpful information to gather

✓ Photos of affected areas, when safe

✓ Date and time the damage was discovered

✓ Notes about steps already taken

✓ Your insurer’s claim number, if available
