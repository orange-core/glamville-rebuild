INSURANCE & DOCUMENTATION

Know what to record and what to ask.

Property damage can mean calls and paperwork. Use this general checklist to prepare for a conversation with your insurer and a restoration provider.

Open the checklist ↓Contact the company ↗
BEFORE YOU CALL

Prepare information you already have.

Only document damage when it is safe. Never put yourself at risk to take photos or collect belongings.

STEP 01

Record the incident

Note when you discovered damage, what you observed and steps already taken to reduce further damage.

STEP 02

Save supporting records

Keep safe photos, receipts, relevant messages, estimates and a list of affected belongings.

STEP 03

Keep claim details together

Record insurer contact information, claim number and dates of calls.

STEP 04

Ask before discarding items

Ask your insurer how to document damaged property before disposing of it.

QUESTIONS TO ASK

Clarify the process with your insurer.

Your insurer can explain your policy, claim process and any required approvals. Keep notes and request written confirmation when appropriate.

  • What does my policy cover for this type of damage?
  • What deductible or out-of-pocket costs may apply?
  • Are there preferred vendors or approval steps?
  • What records are needed before work starts?
  • How should urgent protective work be documented?
Important: Restoration work and insurance claims are separate decisions. This guide is general information, not legal advice, a coverage determination or a promise that a claim will be approved. Confirm your policy terms with your insurer.
