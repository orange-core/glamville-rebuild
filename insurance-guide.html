CLAIM PREPARATION

A simple checklist to get organised.

Follow your insurer’s instructions and keep copies of relevant communication.

01

Record the damage safely

Take photos or video when safe, note when the damage was discovered and list affected areas and belongings.

02

Contact your insurer

Ask about your policy, deductible, claim number, urgent protective measures, approved vendors and deadlines.

03

Keep estimates and receipts

Save communications, estimates, invoices and receipts. Ask who is responsible for approvals before work begins.
