INSURANCE & DOCUMENTATION

Make the claims conversation easier to organise.

Property damage can create a lot to keep track of. This general checklist can help you gather information and prepare questions for your insurer while you confirm repair and restoration options.

YOUR DOCUMENTATION CHECKLIST

Keep the details in one place.

Only document or photograph damage when it is safe to do so. Follow official guidance and your insurer’s instructions.

Record the incident

Write down when the damage was discovered, what happened as far as you know, and which areas appear affected.

Take safe photographs

Capture visible damage from a safe location. Do not enter unstable or hazardous areas to get a picture.

Contact your insurer

Ask about your policy, deductible, claim number, required documentation and any preferred contractor process.

Keep estimates and receipts

Organise estimates, invoices, receipts and communications. Ask before committing to work if you are unsure what is authorised.

QUESTIONS TO ASK

Confirm the process before making assumptions.

Coverage and claim decisions depend on your policy and insurer. A restoration provider cannot guarantee that a claim will be approved.

  • What does my policy say about this type of damage?
  • Do I need an inspection or approval before work?
  • What documentation is required?
  • How are estimates, deductibles and payment handled?
  • Who should I contact if the scope changes?
