Water damage needs a clear next step.

WHAT TO EXPECT

A thoughtful process from first contact to recovery.

Every property and water event is different. This page explains common discussion points; the actual inspection, scope and services must be confirmed with the restoration provider.

STEP 01

Address immediate safety

Stay clear of standing water near electrical hazards or structural damage. If it is safe to do so, stop the source of water; contact the appropriate utility or emergency service when needed.

STEP 02

Document what you notice

When safe, take photos and notes of affected rooms and belongings. Record when the issue was discovered and keep receipts for urgent protective measures.

STEP 03

Discuss inspection and drying

Ask about the inspection process, moisture assessment, extraction and drying equipment, expected timeline and how progress will be communicated.

Safety first: Do not enter areas with electrical hazards, unstable ceilings or contaminated floodwater. Follow local emergency guidance and your insurer’s instructions. This page is general information, not professional safety, insurance or legal advice.
