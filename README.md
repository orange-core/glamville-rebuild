# Wesconnett Restoration — OrangeCore website concept

Responsive, static eight-page prototype for a Jacksonville, Florida property restoration company.

## Eight-page sitemap
1. `index.html` — homepage / rapid-conversion hub
2. `water-damage.html` — water damage mitigation information
3. `fire-smoke.html` — fire and smoke restoration
4. `mold-remediation.html` — mold and moisture concerns
5. `commercial-restoration.html` — commercial restoration
6. `about.html` — about the company concept
7. `insurance-guide.html` — insurance claims guide
8. `contact.html` — contact and enquiry form

Shared assets: `css/main.css` and `js/main.js`.

## Demo verification notes
- This is a concept preview, not the business's official website.
- Contact numbers, service area, hours, company history, qualifications, response times and insurance practices must be verified with the owner before launch.
- No unsupported promise of arrival within 60 minutes, licensing, certification, response rate or direct insurance billing is made.
- The enquiry form does not submit to a server or send an email. It prepares/copies a message locally.
- External Unsplash images are placeholders and should be replaced with approved business photography.
- Test all pages and links in a browser before sharing the live preview.
